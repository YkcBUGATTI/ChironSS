Chiron Family · 500 Units

Chiron, Specialised for Top Speed

The Chiron (420 km/h) and the Chiron Super Sport (440 km/h) share the W16 heart and all-wheel drive — but the Super Sport answers "can extreme speed and comfort coexist" with a longer tail, more power and lower drag. Grand Tourisme, established.

"Form follows performance" — from the front splitter to the rear diffuser, every centimetre of the skin is designed for top speed.

Power & ControlEngineering

From a W16 strengthened to 1,600 PS, to every tyre at 440 km/h — engineering makes power controllable.

W16 · THE PINNACLE OF POWER

W16: The Pinnacle of Power

8.0 litres, 16 cylinders, four turbochargers — more than 3,500 parts assembled entirely by hand. First fired in 2001 at 1,000+1 PS, it evolved over two decades: 1,000 PS in the Veyron, 1,200 PS in the Super Sport, 1,500 PS in the Chiron and 1,600 PS in the Super Sport. An asymmetric 45-degree firing order gives it a voice like no other.

The Heart · W16

The 1,600 PS Heart

8.0 litres, 16 cylinders, four turbochargers — no larger than a V12, weighing about 400 kg, with more than 3,500 parts assembled entirely by hand. From the Veyron's 1,000 PS to the Super Sport's 1,600 PS, this engine defined Bugatti's era of extreme speed.

The RecordsThe Records

On 2 August 2019, Bugatti became the first manufacturer to break the 300 mph barrier.

02.08.2019 · EHRA-LESSIEN · GERMANY

304.773 MPH — 490.484 KM/H

A near-production Chiron prototype became the first production-based car past 300 mph. A world record.

"We have become the first manufacturer to reach the magic 300-mile-per-hour mark — making ourselves, the entire team and myself, incredibly proud."
The Run · Record Drive

From 300 km/h, in Steps of 50

Andy Wallace worked his way up from 300 km/h in 50 km/h increments to confirm the aero balance. After one warm-up lap, he exited the north curve at 200 km/h, held full throttle for about 70 seconds on the 8.8-kilometre straight, then braked over two kilometres through the south bend — covering 136 metres every second.

The Track · Why Ehra-Lessien

Low Altitude, the Highest Safety Standard

At just 50 metres above sea level, Ehra-Lessien's denser air is a handicap — above 400 km/h the car runs as if against a wall, and altitude can make up to 25 km/h of difference. Yet it is the only place in the world meeting Bugatti's safety standards: a 21-kilometre three-lane straight lined with crash barriers, rescue services at both ends, and the road cleaned with special mats before the run.

The Day · 01

The Driver

Andy Wallace straps on his helmet — Le Mans winner. In 1998 he set a 391 km/h record in a McLaren F1 on this same straight; today he is about to open a lap at 490 km/h.

The Day · 02

Data Calibration

Engineers check telemetry line by line beside the engine bay — every increment confirmed before the run.

The Day · 03

The Day · 04

A sealed GPS box recorded the run, certified by SGS-TÜV Saar — 304.773 mph enters history.

Sur MesureBespoke

"What you see is what you get. If it looks like hand-drawn sketches, then it must be exactly that." — Every Chiron Super Sport can be unique.

GOLDEN ERA · THE PINNACLE OF HAND-CRAFTED LUXURY

Golden Era: The Pinnacle of Hand-Crafted Luxury

Two artists, 45 hand-drawn sketches and a newly developed 'Doré' gold gradient — over two years, Bugatti's century of history was drawn, line by line, onto a Nocturne-black body.

Sur Mesure · Golden Era

A Hand-Drawn Chronicle of Bugatti

45 hand-drawn sketches: 19 on the driver's side (1987 to today), 26 on the passenger side (1909–1956) — a newly developed 'Doré' gold gradient flowing over Nocturne black, two years in the making.

Sur Mesure · Type 50S Hommage

50177: A Black Legend

A tribute to chassis 50177, the 1931 Le Mans legend: lustrous black, a hand-painted '5' on both flanks, 'Le Mans 1931' script and the engine-turned 'Perlée' finish — even the underside of the rear wing carries a complete map of the 1931 Circuit de la Sarthe.

Sur Mesure · Type 55 '55 1 of 1'

A Two-Tone Tribute

Jean Bugatti's groundbreaking duotone scheme, recreated in black and yellow: a black centreline over the nose, a fading '55' pattern across the fenders, and '55 1 of 1' in cursive under the wing — with Jean's signature on the sill and dedication plate.

L'ULTIME · 2024

The End of the Chiron Era

The 500th and last Chiron. On its French Racing Blue fading to Atlantic Blue, the coordinates of every chapter of the Chiron era are written by hand.
